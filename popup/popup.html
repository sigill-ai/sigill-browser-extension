Sigill Digital Evidence
Recording — your content stays local

Each conversation is recorded as one verifiable run, entirely in your browser:

  1. Every completed answer becomes a step: the prompt, attached files and the answer are hashed with SHA-256 locally.
  2. The step is a small evidence envelope, canonicalised with JCS (RFC 8785), holding those digests and a link to the previous step.
  3. Only the envelope digest is sent to Sigill, which seals it and adds an RFC 3161 timestamp.
  4. A conversation idle for 30 minutes is closed with a final timestamped step, which freezes the run.

Sigill receives digests and the signed envelopes, never your prompts, answers or files. Runs appear under Evidence in Sigill; export one and anyone can verify it at sigill.ai/verify-run.
